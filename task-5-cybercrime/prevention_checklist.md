# Stay Safe Online – Cybercrime Prevention Checklist

**CSE0001 – Digital Literacy | Task 5 | Riddhima Saluja (25BET10005)**

---

A practical checklist for college students in India to stay safe from phishing, financial fraud, and online scams.

---

## 🛡️ General Cyber Safety

1. **Always check the sender's email address carefully before clicking any link.**
   Fraudsters use domains like "scholarships.gov.in-portal.co" that look official but are fake. The real government portal is always at a simple .gov.in address. When in doubt, type the URL directly into your browser.

2. **Never enter your Aadhaar number, bank account details, or OTP on a website you reached through an email or SMS link.**
   Legitimate government scholarship portals, banks, and universities will never ask for sensitive information through unsolicited emails. Always navigate to official websites directly.

3. **Install and keep an antivirus / anti-phishing tool active on your devices.**
   Tools like Windows Defender (free, built-in) and Malwarebytes flag known phishing websites before you can enter any information.

4. **Enable login alerts and notifications on all important accounts.**
   Gmail, banking apps, and LinkedIn can notify you instantly when a new device logs in. Turn these on so you know immediately if your account is compromised.

5. **Be sceptical of any message — email, SMS, or WhatsApp — that creates urgency.**
   Phrases like "Act now or lose your scholarship," "Your account will be blocked in 24 hours," or "Claim your prize before midnight" are classic pressure tactics used by scammers. Legitimate institutions do not communicate this way.

6. **Verify scholarship and job offer information through official channels only.**
   Before responding to any financial offer, visit the official website (typed manually), call the official helpline, or visit the office in person. Never trust a link or phone number provided in an unsolicited message.

---

## 💰 UPI & Financial Safety

7. **Never share your bank account number, IFSC code, or UPI PIN in any online form unless you initiated the process yourself.**
   To receive a scholarship or payment, you only need to provide your bank account number and IFSC — never your UPI PIN. Anyone asking for your PIN is attempting fraud.

8. **Register on NPCI's SMS alert service and check your bank statement weekly.**
   Small unauthorised transactions are often the first sign of identity theft. Catching them early limits the damage and speeds up the recovery process.

---

## 📢 Where to Report Cybercrime in India

| Resource | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **National Cyber Crime Portal** | [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in) – File a complaint online 24×7 |
| **Cyber Crime Helpline** | **1930** – Call 24×7 for urgent financial fraud cases |
| **CERT-In** | cert-in.org.in – Report phishing websites and malicious emails |
| **Local Police** | Visit your nearest police station or file an e-FIR online |

> **Important:** If you have entered your bank details on a suspicious website, call your bank's customer care immediately to freeze the account, and then file a complaint on the 1930 helpline. Speed is critical in financial fraud cases.
