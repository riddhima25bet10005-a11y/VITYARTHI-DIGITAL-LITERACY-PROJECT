## What is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of cybercrime where an attacker disguises themselves as a trustworthy entity — such as a bank, university, government body, or popular website — to trick the victim into revealing sensitive information like passwords, OTPs, or financial details.

---

## Case Study: The Fake Scholarship Email

**Scenario (Realistic Fictional Case)**

Priya, an 18-year-old first-year engineering student in Indore, received an email in September 2025 with the subject line: *"Congratulations! You have been selected for the National Merit Scholarship – Action Required."*

**Step-by-Step: How the Fraud Happened**

1. The email appeared to come from **scholarships.gov.in-merit-portal.co** — a domain that looked official at a glance but was a fraudulent lookalike of the real government portal.
2. The email addressed Priya by name (obtained from a leaked college admission list) and stated she had been selected for a ₹50,000 annual scholarship.
3. She was asked to click a link to "verify her bank details" and submit her Aadhaar number, bank account number, and IFSC code to receive the funds.
4. The link led to a professional-looking fake website that mirrored the National Scholarship Portal design exactly.
5. Priya entered her details. Within hours, ₹12,000 was withdrawn from her account via a linked UPI ID that the attacker had registered using her Aadhaar information.

**Who is Usually Targeted?**

Students, job seekers, and senior citizens — people who are either unfamiliar with how official government portals work, or who are in a financially vulnerable position and respond quickly to financial incentives.

**Consequences**

Financial loss of ₹12,000, identity compromise (Aadhaar-linked fraud), emotional distress, and a lengthy process of filing a complaint on cybercrime.gov.in and coordinating with her bank. Recovery of funds was only partial.
