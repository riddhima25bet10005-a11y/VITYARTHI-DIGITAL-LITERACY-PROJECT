Email Etiquette — Do It Right
  • Write a clear, specific subject line
  • Begin with "Dear Dr./Prof. [Name]"
  • State your purpose in the first sentence
  • Keep it brief and respectful
  • Proofread before hitting Send
  • Sign off with your name + reg. no.
✗ Never use slang, emoji, or "hey" in formal emails
🔐
Safe Internet Practices
  • Never share your OTP — not even with "bank staff"
  • Check for https:// before entering passwords
  • Avoid public Wi-Fi for banking or login pages
  • Log out of shared/college computers
  • Use 2-Factor Authentication on all accounts
  • Download apps only from official stores
