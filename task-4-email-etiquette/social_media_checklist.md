# Social Media Do's and Don'ts for College Students

**CSE0001 – Digital Literacy | Task 4 | Riddhima Saluja (25BET10005)**

---

## ✅ DO's

1. **Keep your bio and profile information professional and up to date.** On platforms like LinkedIn and GitHub, an outdated profile is almost as bad as no profile at all. Review and update every six months.

2. **Use your college email for all academic and professional sign-ups.** This keeps your academic and personal digital lives separate, and many platforms offer student discounts when you sign up with an .ac.in address.

3. **Follow industry professionals and researchers in your field.** LinkedIn and Twitter/X are excellent sources of career advice, research updates, and internship opportunities — but only if your feed is curated thoughtfully.

4. **Watermark or label your original work before sharing it.** If you create a design, write an article, or produce any original content, add your name before posting it so your work cannot be stolen without credit.

5. **Participate in professional communities respectfully.** Whether it is a GitHub Discussion, a Stack Overflow thread, or a LinkedIn comment section, always communicate with kindness, clarity, and patience.

6. **Archive important digital conversations and documents.** Save emails, offer letters, and important messages to Google Drive or a local folder. Relying on platform history alone is risky.

7. **Think of your digital presence as your first impression.** A recruiter who finds your LinkedIn profile or GitHub page before your resume is essentially interviewing you without telling you.

8. **Use strong, unique passwords for every social account and enable 2FA.** A compromised social media account can damage your reputation permanently.

---

## ❌ DON'Ts

1. **Don't post anything you wouldn't say in a classroom.** The internet amplifies tone. A message that felt casual when you typed it can read as rude, insensitive, or unprofessional to someone else.

2. **Don't tag people in photos without their permission.** Especially in a college setting, always ask before tagging classmates, professors, or friends in any post or story.

3. **Don't share personal academic records, ID cards, or certificates as posts.** Roll number, Aadhaar details, or fee receipts shared casually can be misused by identity thieves.

4. **Don't rely on social media as your only source of news.** Cross-check information with reliable sources (government portals, reputed news organisations) before believing or sharing it.

5. **Don't use college or batch group chats to share unverified information.** WhatsApp groups are especially prone to misinformation. Verify before forwarding — always.

6. **Don't respond to messages from strangers offering money, prizes, or "easy job" opportunities.** These are almost always scams. Legitimate recruiters do not approach candidates through random DMs.

7. **Don't post during lectures, exams, or official college events.** It reflects poorly on your discipline and can be noticed by faculty.

8. **Don't use the same profile picture across anonymous and professional accounts.** Reverse image searches can connect accounts you intended to keep separate.
