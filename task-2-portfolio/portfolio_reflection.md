### 1. GitHub (https://github.com/riddhima25bet10005-a11y)
GitHub is the world's largest platform for version control and collaborative software development. It allows developers and students to host code repositories, track changes over time, and showcase their work to the world. I created a **profile README** — a special public repository named after my username — containing my name, branch, year, and a brief introduction: *"I am a first-year Electronics & Technology student at VIT Bhopal, looking forward to learning embedded systems, Python, and exploring the intersection of hardware and software."*

### 3. Stack Overflow (https://stackoverflow.com/users/32551141/riddhima-saluja)
Stack Overflow is the world's largest Q&A community for programmers and developers. It is widely used to find solutions to coding problems, understand error messages, and learn from the experience of the global developer community. Even as a beginner, having a Stack Overflow account encourages me to engage with technical questions, learn how to ask well-formed questions, and eventually contribute answers as my skills grow.

## How I Plan to Use These Platforms Over Four Years

Over the next four years, I plan to build a consistent presence across all three platforms. On **GitHub**, I will commit my semester lab assignments from the very first month so that my contribution graph reflects consistent activity. By my third year, I aim to have at least one personal project related to IoT or embedded systems publicly visible. On **LinkedIn**, I will update my profile after every internship, workshop, and certification. I also plan to write at least one LinkedIn post per semester sharing what I have learned. On **Stack Overflow**, I will begin by searching for solutions to problems I face in lab sessions, and by second year, I hope to start contributing answers to beginner-level questions in Python and C.
